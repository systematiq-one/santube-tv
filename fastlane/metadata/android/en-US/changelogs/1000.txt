* First SanTube build for TV, based on SmartTube 32.81. It installs next to SmartTube as its own app
* SanTube look: dark tonal colors with red accents on the sidebar, cards, dialogs and progress bars, and a red atom loading animation. The other color schemes are still in Settings
* The captions button turns on the captions in the video's own language (English as a fallback); hold it for the full list
* A song played on its own continues with YouTube's mix for that song
* Sign-in no longer fails when the connection drops while you enter the code
* No in-app updates yet: new versions are published on this page
